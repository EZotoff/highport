### Accessible Text Hierarchy Tokens
Added new semantic text tokens to `apps/web/app/design-tokens.css`:
- `--text-heading`: High contrast for emphasis (#f1f5f9, 15.6:1)
- `--text-label`: For captions and labels (#cbd5e1, 11.0:1)
- `--text-body`: Alias for primary text (#e2e8f0, 13.8:1)
- `--text-subtle`: Alias for secondary text (#94a3b8, 6.0:1)

Documented contrast ratios for WCAG AA compliance against `--deep-void` background.
